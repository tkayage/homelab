## Threat Model

<threat_model>
- **T-05-01**: LXC container uses default privileged mode. Mitigated by `unprivileged = true`.
- **T-05-02**: Unbounded WAL growth due to stalled replication slots. Mitigated by `max_slot_wal_keep_size = 4GB`.
- **T-05-03**: Network exposure to untrusted LAN clients. Mitigated by binding to `10.10.30.100` and limiting `pg_hba.conf` to `10.10.30.0/24`.
</threat_model>
